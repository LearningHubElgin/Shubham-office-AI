FAQ

Questions about pricing

Is GST included in the price?

No. Every price is in rupees before GST, and 18% GST is added when you pay: Clinic Basic, for example, is ₹11,999 a year + GST. You buy from Magizh NexGen Technologies, GSTIN 33EACPR6502N1ZH, and get a GST invoice by email for every payment.

Are plans billed monthly or yearly?

Yearly. Every plan is priced and billed by the year, in rupees plus GST. After the free trial, Razorpay takes the year’s payment by auto-debit, and the plan renews the same way each year until you cancel. Each plan shows its yearly price, the users it includes and, with the AI agent, its AI credits.

How does the free trial work?

You pay nothing until it ends. You approve auto-debit with Razorpay when you start, and the first payment is taken when the trial ends. Trials run 7 days on Clinic, 7 on Coaching, 7 on Turf, 14 on Real Estate, 14 on Gym, 14 on Salon and 14 on Field Service. Cancel before the end and you pay nothing.

Are WhatsApp message charges included?

No. iSuite AI uses Meta’s official WhatsApp Business Platform, and Meta bills its message charges to your own WhatsApp Business account, at Meta’s rates, separately from your iSuite AI plan. Meta’s rates depend on the kind of message and the country; see Meta’s WhatsApp pricing.

What are AI credits, and how many come with a plan?

AI credits pay for the AI agent’s work, in rupees. Every plan with the AI agent includes free credits — a month’s or a year’s worth, shown on each plan — used first. Salon Basic and Field Service Basic have no AI agent. Need more? Top up in the app; credits you buy never expire.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. A bigger plan starts at once: its features and users switch on straight away, and you pay the difference, plus GST, for the rest of the period. A smaller plan starts at your next renewal, and the current period is not partly refunded. The owner or an admin changes the plan in the app.

Can I cancel, and is there a refund?

You can cancel at any time by telling us on WhatsApp or by email, and your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for. Payments are not refunded once a plan period has started, except in the cases our refund and cancellation policy lists.
