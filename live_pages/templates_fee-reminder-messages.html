Fees reminder message to parents: free templates to copy

Every fees reminder message to parents a coaching centre or tuition class needs: before, on and after the due date, instalments, part payments, receipts, a fee hike notice and a holiday version, plus messages for adult students. Each is in English, Tamil, Tanglish and Hindi. Change the words in [brackets], then send.

What to put in a fees reminder message to parents

A parent should be able to read the message once and pay. That needs six things, in this order, and nothing more.

Put inWhyWrite it like this
The parent’s nameA named message is read; “Dear Parent” is skipped.Lakshmi madam, Mr Rahman
The student’s name and batchParents with two children at your centre need to know which fee it is.Arjun, NEET evening batch
The amount still owedOnly what is left, never the full year’s fee after a part is paid.₹12,000 (not “Rs.12000/-”)
The due dateWith the month in words, so nobody reads 02/11 as 11 February.Monday, 2 November
How to payThe step people get stuck on. Give the UPI ID, the link or the office hours.UPI to yourname@upi, or the office, 10 am–6 pm
An “already paid?” lineSaves an angry call from the parent who paid yesterday.If you have already paid, please ignore this.

Leave out: other students’ names, marks or results, threats, a list of who has not paid, red warning emojis and capital letters. Name your institute in the first reminder of each round, as the before-the-due-date message does; a parent may have children at two different classes. Never ask for an OTP, a card number or a UPI PIN: a parent paying you does not need to share any of them, and saying so once at admission helps parents spot a fake fee message. If you charge GST on fees, say whether the amount includes it, and check with your CA how it should be shown.

When to send each one

One message per step, sent in the morning on a working day, and the steps stop the day the parent pays. The later steps are only for those who still have not.

WhenMessageTone
3 to 7 days beforeBefore the due dateA friendly heads-up
On the day, morningOn the due dateClear, with how to pay
3 days afterGentleAssume they forgot
7 days afterFirmA date to pay by, and an offer to split it
15 days afterFinalAsk for a call; your written policy, if any
When money comes inPart payment or receiptThanks, and the balance
A month before a changeFee hike noticeThe reason and the date it starts
Before a holiday or examsHoliday and exam timeEarly, so it is off their list

Avoid nights, Sundays and festival days. Before every send, check the day’s payments, so nobody who paid at the desk this morning gets an “overdue” message at noon.

Before the due date

Send 3 to 7 days ahead, so the family has time to plan for it.

English

Dear [Parent name],
A gentle reminder from [Institute name]: [Student name]’s [Batch] fee of ₹[Amount] is due on [Due date].
You can pay by [How to pay].
If you have already paid, please ignore this message. Thank you!

வணக்கம் [Parent name],
[Institute name] சார்பில் ஒரு நினைவூட்டல்: உங்கள் பிள்ளை [Student name]-இன் [Batch] கட்டணம் ₹[Amount]. செலுத்த வேண்டிய நாள்: [Due date].
[How to pay] மூலம் செலுத்தலாம்.
ஏற்கனவே செலுத்தியிருந்தால், இந்தச் செய்தியைப் பொருட்படுத்த வேண்டாம். நன்றி!

Tanglish

Vanakkam [Parent name],
[Institute name] la irundhu oru reminder: [Student name] oda [Batch] fees ₹[Amount], [Due date] kulla kattanum.
[How to pay] la pay pannalaam.

नमस्ते [Parent name] जी,
[Institute name] की ओर से एक रिमाइंडर: [Student name] की [Batch] फीस ₹[Amount] है, जिसे [Due date] तक जमा करना है।
आप [How to pay] से भुगतान कर सकते हैं।
अगर आप पहले ही भुगतान कर चुके हैं, तो कृपया इस संदेश को अनदेखा करें। धन्यवाद!

On the due date

Send in the morning, so there is a whole day to pay.

English

Dear [Parent name],
Today, [Due date], is the last date for [Student name]’s [Batch] fee of ₹[Amount].
Please pay today by [How to pay].
Already paid? Please send us the receipt here and we will update our records. Thank you.

Tamil · தமிழ்

உங்கள் பிள்ளை [Student name]-இன் [Batch] கட்டணம் ₹[Amount] செலுத்த இன்றுதான் ([Due date]) கடைசி நாள்.
இன்றே [How to pay] மூலம் செலுத்திவிடுங்கள்.
ஏற்கனவே செலுத்தியிருந்தால், ரசீதை இங்கே அனுப்புங்கள்; எங்கள் பதிவில் சரிசெய்து கொள்கிறோம். நன்றி.

Tanglish

Vanakkam [Parent name],
[Student name] oda [Batch] fees ₹[Amount] kattradhukku innaikku ([Due date]) dhaan last date.
Innaikke [How to pay] la kattidunga.
Already kattiyirundha, receipt-a inga anuppunga, naanga update pannikirom. Nandri.

Hindi · हिंदी

नमस्ते [Parent name] जी,
[Student name] की [Batch] फीस ₹[Amount] जमा करने की आज ([Due date]) अंतिम तिथि है।
कृपया आज ही [How to pay] से भुगतान कर दें।
अगर भुगतान हो चुका है, तो रसीद यहाँ भेज दें, हम अपना रिकॉर्ड ठीक कर लेंगे। धन्यवाद।

After the due date: gentle

Three days late, assume the parent forgot, and offer help in the same message.

English

Dear [Parent name],
[Student name]’s [Batch] fee of ₹[Amount], due on [Due date], is still pending. It may have slipped your mind.
You can pay by [How to pay].
If something is making it hard to pay right now, just reply here and we will work it out together.

Tamil · தமிழ்

வணக்கம் [Parent name],
[Due date] அன்று செலுத்த வேண்டிய [Student name]-இன் [Batch] கட்டணம் ₹[Amount] இன்னும் நிலுவையில் உள்ளது. ஒருவேளை மறந்திருக்கலாம்.
[How to pay] மூலம் செலுத்தலாம்.
இப்போது செலுத்துவதில் சிரமம் இருந்தால், இங்கே பதில் அனுப்புங்கள்; சேர்ந்து ஒரு வழி பார்க்கலாம்.

Tanglish

Vanakkam [Parent name],
[Due date] kulla katta vendiya [Student name] oda [Batch] fees ₹[Amount] innum pending-la irukku. Marandhirukkalaam.
[How to pay] la kattalaam.
Ippo katta edhavadhu kashtam irundha, inga reply pannunga, serndhu oru vazhi paarkalaam.

Hindi · हिंदी

नमस्ते [Parent name] जी,
[Student name] की [Batch] फीस ₹[Amount], जो [Due date] को जमा होनी थी, अभी बाकी है। शायद ध्यान से छूट गया हो।
आप [How to pay] से भुगतान कर सकते हैं।
अगर अभी भुगतान में कोई दिक्कत है, तो यहीं जवाब दें, हम मिलकर कोई रास्ता निकाल लेंगे।

After the due date: firm

A week late, give a clear date to pay by and a way out: paying in parts. Firm is about being specific, not sharp.

English

Dear [Parent name],
[Student name]’s [Batch] fee of ₹[Amount] was due on [Due date] and is now [X] days late.
Please pay by [New date] through [How to pay].
If you would like to pay it in parts, reply today and we will fix a plan with you.

Tamil · தமிழ்

வணக்கம் [Parent name],
[Student name]-இன் [Batch] கட்டணம் ₹[Amount], [Due date] அன்று செலுத்த வேண்டியது. இப்போது [X] நாட்கள் தாமதமாகிவிட்டது.
தயவுசெய்து [New date]-க்குள் [How to pay] மூலம் செலுத்துங்கள்.
பகுதி பகுதியாகச் செலுத்த விரும்பினால், இன்றே பதில் அனுப்புங்கள்; உங்களுடன் பேசி ஒரு திட்டம் அமைக்கிறோம்.

Tanglish

Vanakkam [Parent name],
[Student name] oda [Batch] fees ₹[Amount], [Due date] annaike katta vendiyadhu. Ippo [X] naal late aayiduchu.
[New date] kulla [How to pay] la please kattidunga.
Konjam konjama katta virumbuna, innaikke reply pannunga, ungakitta pesi oru plan pottudalaam.

Hindi · हिंदी

नमस्ते [Parent name] जी,
[Student name] की [Batch] फीस ₹[Amount] [Due date] को जमा होनी थी, और अब [X] दिन से बकाया है।
कृपया [New date] तक [How to pay] से भुगतान करें।
अगर आप थोड़ा-थोड़ा करके भुगतान करना चाहें, तो आज ही जवाब दें, हम आपके साथ मिलकर योजना बना लेंगे।

Charge a late fee? Add one line, only if it is in the fee terms the parent signed at admission: “As per our fee terms, a late fee of ₹[Late fee] applies from [Date].” The late-fee, policy and fee-hike lines on this page are samples to adapt with your lawyer.

Final reminder

Two weeks late, ask for a call. A call tells you what no message can: whether the family needs time, or has left.

English

Dear [Parent name],
This is our final reminder about [Student name]’s [Batch] fee of ₹[Amount], pending since [Due date].
Please pay by [Last date] through [How to pay], or call us on [Phone] so we can agree on a plan.
[Your policy line, if any.]
We want [Student name] to keep learning with us, and would like to sort this out with you.

Tamil · தமிழ்

வணக்கம் [Parent name],
[Due date] முதல் நிலுவையில் உள்ள [Student name]-இன் [Batch] கட்டணம் ₹[Amount] குறித்த எங்கள் இறுதி நினைவூட்டல் இது.
தயவுசெய்து [Last date]-க்குள் [How to pay] மூலம் செலுத்துங்கள், அல்லது [Phone] எண்ணில் அழைத்துப் பேசுங்கள்; சேர்ந்து தீர்வு காணலாம்.
[Your policy line, if any.]
[Student name] எங்களிடம் தொடர்ந்து படிக்க வேண்டும் என்பதே எங்கள் விருப்பம்.

Tanglish

Vanakkam [Parent name],
[Due date] la irundhu pending-la irukura [Student name] oda [Batch] fees ₹[Amount] pathi idhu engaloda final reminder.
[Last date] kulla [How to pay] la kattidunga, illa [Phone] ku call pannunga, pesi oru vazhi paarkalaam.
[Your policy line, if any.]
[Student name] engakitta thodarndhu padikkanum-nu dhaan naanga aasaipadrom.

Hindi · हिंदी

नमस्ते [Parent name] जी,
[Student name] की [Batch] फीस ₹[Amount], जो [Due date] से बकाया है, उसके बारे में यह हमारा अंतिम रिमाइंडर है।
कृपया [Last date] तक [How to pay] से भुगतान करें, या [Phone] पर कॉल करें ताकि हम मिलकर हल निकाल सकें।
[Your policy line, if any.]
हम चाहते हैं कि [Student name] हमारे साथ पढ़ाई जारी रखे।

The policy line is for a rule you have in writing and actually follow, for example “Study material for the next unit is given once the fee is cleared.” Do not threaten legal action, and do not tie the fee to a child’s marks, hall ticket or results in a message; if it comes to that, talk to the parent in person.

Instalment reminder

For fees paid in parts. Showing what is already paid reassures the parent that their earlier payments were counted.

English

Dear [Parent name],
Instalment [2] of [4] for [Student name]’s [Course] is due on [Due date].
Amount: ₹[Amount]
Paid so far: ₹[Paid] of ₹[Total fee]
You can pay by [How to pay]. Thank you for keeping to the schedule!

Tamil · தமிழ்

வணக்கம் [Parent name],
[Student name]-இன் [Course] கட்டணத்தின் [4] தவணைகளில் [2]-ஆவது தவணையை [Due date] அன்று செலுத்த வேண்டும்.
தொகை: ₹[Amount]
இதுவரை செலுத்தியது: ₹[Total fee]-இல் ₹[Paid]
[How to pay] மூலம் செலுத்தலாம். தவணைகளைச் சரியான நேரத்தில் செலுத்துவதற்கு நன்றி!

Tanglish

Vanakkam [Parent name],
[Student name] oda [Course] fees-la [4] installment-la [2]-vadhu installment [Due date] annaike kattanum.
Amount: ₹[Amount]
Ippo varaikkum kattinadhu: ₹[Total fee]-la ₹[Paid]
[How to pay] la kattalaam. Correct time-ku kattradhukku nandri!

Hindi · हिंदी

नमस्ते [Parent name] जी,
[Student name] की [Course] फीस की किस्त [2] (कुल [4] में से) [Due date] तक जमा करनी है।
राशि: ₹[Amount]
अब तक जमा: ₹[Total fee] में से ₹[Paid]
आप [How to pay] से भुगतान कर सकते हैं। किस्तें समय पर देने के लिए धन्यवाद!

Part payment received

When a parent pays only some of it. Thank them first, then the balance and its date, so the next reminder is no surprise.

English

Dear [Parent name],
Thank you! We have received ₹[Paid now] towards [Student name]’s [Batch] fee on [Date].
Balance: ₹[Balance], to be paid by [Due date].
We will remind you a few days before. Please keep this message as your note of the payment.

மீதித் தொகை: ₹[Balance]. இதை [Due date]-க்குள் செலுத்த வேண்டும்.

Thank-you and receipt lines

Short lines for the moment the money comes in: one with the receipt details, one for a fee paid in full. A WhatsApp line is a courtesy, not your accounts. Keep issuing the receipt or invoice your CA asks for.

English

Received with thanks: ₹[Amount] towards [Student name]’s [Batch] fee on [Date], by [Cash / UPI / card / cheque]. Receipt no. [Number]. Balance: ₹[Balance].

Thank you, [Parent name]! [Student name]’s [Batch] fee is now paid in full. Nothing more is due.

Nandri! [Student name] oda [Batch] fees ₹[Amount] [Date] annaike [Cash / UPI / card / cheque] la vandhuduchu. Receipt no. [Number]. Meedhi: ₹[Balance].

Nandri [Parent name]! [Student name] oda [Batch] fees full-a kattiyaachu. Ini edhuvum meedhi illa.

धन्यवाद सहित प्राप्त: [Student name] की [Batch] फीस ₹[Amount], [Date] को, [Cash / UPI / card / cheque] से। रसीद नं.: [Number]। बाकी: ₹[Balance]।

धन्यवाद, [Parent name] जी! [Student name] की [Batch] फीस पूरी जमा हो गई है। अब कुछ भी बाकी नहीं है।

Fee hike notice

Send it at least a month before the new fee starts, with one honest reason. Say plainly what happens to fees already agreed. A change told early is easier to accept than one found on a bill.

English

Dear [Parent name],
A notice from [Institute name]: from [Effective date], the fee for [Course] will be ₹[New fee] (now ₹[Old fee]).
The reason: [one line — e.g. new printed material and an extra weekly test].
The instalments already set for [Student name] stay the same until [Date].
For any question, reply here or meet us at the office.

Tamil · தமிழ்

வணக்கம் [Parent name],
[Institute name] அறிவிப்பு: [Effective date] முதல் [Course] கட்டணம் ₹[New fee] ஆக மாறுகிறது (இப்போது ₹[Old fee]).
காரணம்: [ஒரு வரியில் — எ.கா. புதிய பாடப் புத்தகங்கள், வாரம் ஒரு கூடுதல் தேர்வு].
[Student name]-க்கு ஏற்கனவே முடிவான தவணைகள் [Date] வரை மாறாது.
சந்தேகம் இருந்தால், இங்கே பதில் அனுப்புங்கள் அல்லது அலுவலகத்தில் நேரில் சந்தியுங்கள்.

Tanglish

Polite, but clear: tone tips

  • Lead with the child, not the debt. “Arjun’s NEET fee” reads as care; “Your dues” reads as a bill.
  • Say “pending” before you say “late”. The first two messages should never use “overdue” or “defaulter”.
  • One message per step. Three messages in a day feel like pressure, and parents mute the chat.
  • Always give a way out. Paying in parts, a call, a few more days. Assume a tight week before you assume a refusal.
  • Never in a group. Do not post pending fees in a batch or parents’ group, and never name a student who has not paid.
  • Never through the child. Do not remind a minor in class or ask them to “tell your parents”. Message the parent.
  • Thank them every time they pay. A thank-you line makes the next reminder easier to send and to receive.
  • Write in the parent’s language. Use the language they write to you in: Tamil, Tanglish, Hindi or English.

Fee reminders on the WhatsApp Business API

From the WhatsApp Business app, each reminder is typed or pasted by hand, because every parent’s name and amount are different. To send them automatically, an institute uses Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform (the API), and its rules shape the message.

Templates, and the 24-hour window

When a parent messages you, a 24-hour customer service window opens, and each new message from them restarts it. Inside it you can reply in any words. Outside it, which is when most reminders go, you may only send a message template that Meta has approved.

Utility or marketing

Every template has a category. Utility is for messages with no promotion in them that are specific to the person, such as an update on their own account; Meta’s own utility examples include a reminder that a monthly payment will be billed. A plain fee reminder to a student you have admitted fits that description. Marketing covers everything promotional, and Meta lists festival greetings and birthday wishes among its marketing examples.

  • Keep offers out. A fee reminder with “Refer a friend and get 10% off” is mixed content, and Meta treats mixed content as marketing.
  • Meta re-checks. It reviews approved templates on its own and can move a utility template to marketing, usually with a day’s notice. You can ask for a review within 60 days, starting in WhatsApp Manager.
  • Messages are charged. Meta charges per template message delivered, at a rate set by its category and the parent’s country; see Meta’s WhatsApp pricing for today’s rates.

Variables, the blanks Meta fills

In a template, the parts that change are numbered: {{1}}, {{2}} and so on, in order from 1, with no gaps. Meta also accepts named ones in lowercase with underscores, such as {{student_name}}. Meta rejects a template that:

  • starts or ends with a variable (“{{1}}, your fee is due” is refused);
  • has gaps in the numbering, or symbols such as #, $ or % inside the braces;
  • has too many variables for how short it is: keep real words around them;
  • threatens the reader, for example with legal action.

Every variable needs a sample value when you submit, so Meta can see what the message will look like.

Consent and approval

  • Opt-in first. Meta asks that people agree to get your messages, and the agreement must name your institute. The easiest place is the admission form: one line and a tick for fee, class and attendance updates on WhatsApp.
  • Let them stop. Tell parents how to stop a kind of message, and stop it when they ask. Meta slows down numbers whose quality stays low.
  • Approval. Meta reviews each template, which can take up to 24 hours. Only an approved template can be sent.
  • Language. Choose the language the words are in: Tamil is ta, Hindi hi, English en or en_US. Meta has no separate language for Tanglish, so send the Tamil or English template, and use Tanglish when you reply inside the 24-hour window.

FAQ

Questions about fee reminders

How do I write a polite fees reminder message to parents?

Start with the parent’s name and the student’s name, then give the batch, the amount still owed, the due date and exactly how to pay. Add a line for those who have already paid, and offer help if paying is hard right now. Keep it short, write it in the parent’s language, and never send it to a group.

When should a coaching centre send fee reminders?

Send a friendly reminder 3 to 7 days before the due date and another on the day. If the fee is unpaid, follow up gently after 3 days, firmly after a week, and with a final message asking for a call after two weeks. Send each in the morning on a working day, and stop once the parent pays.

Can I send fee reminders in Tamil or Hindi on WhatsApp?

Yes. From the WhatsApp Business app, type or paste any language. On the WhatsApp Business API, submit the template in Tamil (language code ta) or Hindi (hi), and Meta reviews it like any other. Meta has no separate language for Tanglish, so use the Tamil or English template and reply in Tanglish inside the 24-hour window.

Is a fee reminder a utility or a marketing template?

A plain fee reminder to a student you have admitted fits Meta’s utility category, which covers non-promotional messages about something the person is already part of. Add an offer, a referral line or a festival greeting and Meta treats it as marketing. Meta re-checks approved templates and can change the category, so keep reminders free of promotion.

What should a final fee reminder say?

Say plainly that it is the final reminder, give the amount, how long it has been pending, a last date and how to pay, and ask the parent to call. Mention a consequence only if it is in your written fee terms and you follow it. Do not threaten legal action; Meta also rejects templates that threaten readers.

Should a fee reminder carry a UPI ID or a payment link?

Use whichever your parents find easiest. A UPI ID is familiar and quick to pay from any UPI app; a payment link fills in the exact amount, and fee software can match it to the student. Either way, never ask for an OTP, a card number or a UPI PIN, and tell parents at admission that you never will.

Can fee reminders go out automatically?

Yes, with software on the WhatsApp Business API. iSuite AI – Coaching Institute reminds each instalment before, on and after its due date, to the student, the parents or both. On Coaching Basic the reminder says how to pay in your words; on Coaching Growth it carries a Razorpay payment link.
