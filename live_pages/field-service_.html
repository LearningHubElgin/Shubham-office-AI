Careful with customers

Where it fits

iSuite AI – Field Service & AMC keeps your jobs, your technicians’ work, customers and their equipment, the conversations with customers and, on Growth, their contracts. Every plan makes GST invoices and records their payments; on Field Service Growth, Purchases & books adds expenses and suppliers’ bills, with GSTR-1 and Tally exports for your accountant.

What the AI is told never to do

  • Promise a repair price before the technician has seen it
  • Promise an exact time — only the window
  • Give electrical or gas safety advice beyond “switch off and keep away”
  • Offer a discount, or share another customer’s details

FAQ

Field service software: questions service companies ask

What is field service management software?

Field service management software runs the work your technicians do at customers’ homes and sites: taking the complaint, booking a time window, assigning a technician, tracking the job, billing and collecting payment. iSuite AI – Field Service & AMC adds each customer’s equipment and warranty and, on Field Service Growth, AMC contracts and an AI that books jobs.

Which field service software suits a small AC or RO service company in India?

Pick one made for Indian service work: jobs booked in time windows, an Android app for technicians, GST bills with UPI or payment links, and a warranty list. iSuite AI – Field Service & AMC does this from ₹9,999 a year + GST for 5 users; AMC contracts and the AI come with Field Service Growth.

How much does field service management software cost in India?

iSuite AI – Field Service & AMC costs from ₹9,999 a year + GST for your company, with users included. Field Service Basic is ₹9,999 a year + GST with 5 users; Field Service Growth is ₹14,999 a year + GST with 10 users, AMC contracts and the AI receptionist. Both have a 14-day free trial.

What does Field Service Growth add to Field Service Basic?

Field Service Growth adds AMC contracts with their visits and renewals, the AI receptionist that books jobs, reminders that go on their own, quotations, Purchases & books with GSTR-1 and Tally exports, automations, Meta lead ads, forms, email campaigns and team reports. It has 10 users instead of 5, and AI credits every month.

How do AMC contracts work in iSuite AI?

On Field Service Growth, you sell a plan, such as AC maintenance with four visits a year, for the units it covers, with a GST invoice and a payment link. The visit dates are spread across the year for you. Each visit shows on the due list two weeks ahead, and one tap books it as a job.

Can a technician make a GST bill and collect payment at the customer’s place?

Yes, on both plans. In the app the technician adds parts from your store and labour, makes a numbered bill with GST when you charge it, and records cash, UPI or card, or sends a Razorpay payment link. Parts come off your stock, the office sees what is owed, and the Done message shows any amount due.

Can customers book an AC or RO service request on WhatsApp?

Yes, on Field Service Growth. The AI receptionist asks what is wrong, the full address, and the day and window, then books the job and replies with the job number. It is told never to promise an exact time, or a price before the technician sees it. On Field Service Basic, your team reads the chat and books the job.

Does iSuite AI track technicians’ live GPS location?

No, iSuite AI does not track live GPS location. Each technician moves their own job along, On the way, Working and Done, and the office board shows it as it happens. The customer gets WhatsApp updates with the technician’s name and number, and the technician gets the customer’s address with a map link.

Is there a technician app for Android?

Yes. Technicians sign in to the iSuite AI Android app and open My day: today’s jobs in time order with call, map and WhatsApp buttons, the unit and its warranty, before and after photos, work notes and the bill. A new job arrives as an app notification and a WhatsApp. On an iPhone, iSuite AI works in the browser.

Does every technician need a login?

Yes. Each technician signs in with their own login, and with the Technician role sees only their own jobs. Field Service Basic comes with 5 users and Field Service Growth with 10, counting the office and the technicians together, and you can add more users when your team grows.

How does iSuite AI track equipment warranty?

Each customer’s addresses hold their units with kind, brand, model, serial, installed date and warranty end, plus every job done on each. Warranties ending show on a list a month ahead, or as many days as you choose, and the customer can be sent a WhatsApp offering an AMC with a tap; on Growth it can go on its own.

Does iSuite AI work in Tamil?

Yes for your customers, on Field Service Growth. The AI replies in Tamil, Tanglish or English, matching the customer or a language you fix, so “AC cooling aagala, naalaiku varuvangala?” still becomes a booked job. The app’s own screens are in English. iSuite AI is made by Magizh NexGen Technologies in Madurai, Tamil Nadu.

Can I export bills to Tally or file GST returns from it?

You can export on Field Service Growth; the filing stays with your accountant. Every plan makes GST invoices and records their payments. Growth adds Purchases & books, with expenses and suppliers’ bills, a GSTR-1 export in JSON or Excel and a Tally XML export, for your accountant to upload on the GST portal and import into Tally.

Is there a free trial of iSuite AI – Field Service & AMC?

Yes. Every field service plan starts with a 14-day free trial. You approve auto-debit with Razorpay at the start, and the first payment is taken only when the trial ends; cancel before then and you pay nothing. There is no free-forever plan. Meta approves your WhatsApp number separately, usually in 1 to 2 weeks.

Which businesses is iSuite AI – Field Service & AMC made for?

It is set up for AC, RO water purifier and home appliance service companies: job types, morning, afternoon and evening windows, kinds of equipment, technician, dispatcher and service manager roles, and, on Growth, sample AC and RO AMC plans. Other trades that send technicians, such as CCTV or pest control, add their own job types and equipment.
