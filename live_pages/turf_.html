FAQ

Questions turf owners ask

What is turf booking software?

Turf booking software runs a turf’s bookings: every court’s slots on one board, prices by the hour, payments at the desk and online, and a record of each customer. iSuite AI – Turf adds an AI that answers players on WhatsApp, offers free slots with prices, holds one and, with your Razorpay connected, sends the advance link.

How much does turf booking software cost in India?

iSuite AI – Turf has two plans: Turf at ₹6,999 a year + GST, and Turf with Academy, which adds a second user, batches, attendance and memberships, at ₹12,999 a year + GST. Both start with a 7-day free trial. There is no commission on bookings: online advances go straight to your own Razorpay account.

Can players book my turf on WhatsApp?

Yes, on both plans. The AI replies on your WhatsApp number at any hour, offers only free slots with their prices and, with your Razorpay connected, holds the chosen slot for 15 minutes and sends a link to pay the advance. Your number first needs Meta’s approval on the WhatsApp Business Platform, and Meta bills message charges separately.

How do I stop double bookings on my turf?

Put every court on one court board. iSuite AI – Turf refuses a clash before it happens and shows what it clashed with. If your ground splits into halves, booking the whole ground blocks both halves and booking a half blocks the whole ground, so the desk, the booking page and the AI never promise one slot twice.

Can I set peak hour and weekend prices for my turf?

Yes. Each court has a normal rate an hour, a peak rate for the hours you pick (6 to 11 pm, say) and a weekend rate. Every booking is priced slot by slot when it is made, and the board, the booking page and the AI quote the same price. Changing a rate later never reprices old bookings.

How do I take an advance for a turf booking online?

Connect your own Razorpay, switch the court online and choose what players pay: nothing, a fixed advance (₹500 unless you change it) or the full price. The slot is held for 15 minutes while they pay; if they do not pay, it is freed for someone else. Before paying, players are told the advance is not refunded on cancellation.

How can a turf reduce no-shows?

Take an advance online so the best evening slots are paid for, and mark no-shows on the court board. In iSuite AI – Turf, a player with two no-shows in six months is asked the full price instead of the advance when booking online. Players who book with their number also get WhatsApp reminders a day and an hour before.

Can I book a regular team every week?

Yes. Book a team the same day and time every week for 4, 8 or 12 weeks in one go. Each week becomes its own booking with its own reminder. A week already taken, or when the court is shut, is skipped and named. “Cancel this and later weeks” calls off the rest and tells the team once.

Does it work for badminton and pickleball courts?

Yes. Each court has its own slot length, so badminton or pickleball courts can run 30-minute slots while the football ground runs hourly ones. A half-hour slot is priced at half the hourly rate. You can name up to ten sports per court, and players can book one slot or several in a row.

Can I run a cricket or football academy from the same app?

Yes, on Turf with Academy. Set up batches, add players to each and take attendance class by class; absent players’ parents get a WhatsApp message the same day, using the template you pick. Memberships handle monthly fees with renewal reminders, and court bookings and the academy stay in one app. The Turf plan has no academy.

Is there a commission on each booking?

No. iSuite AI – Turf charges a yearly fee for your plan, never a cut of each booking. Advances and full payments taken online go straight into your own Razorpay account, which charges its normal gateway fee. Meta bills WhatsApp messages separately, and if the AI uses up its included credits, you recharge in the app.

Can I list my turf on Playo or Hudle and still use iSuite AI – Turf?

Yes, but iSuite AI – Turf does not connect to Playo, Hudle or any other marketplace today. Enter each marketplace booking on the court board as it comes in. The board then blocks that slot, so the desk, the AI and your booking page never offer it to anyone else.

Do I need my own turf booking app for players?

No. Players do not download anything. Each court you switch online gets its own booking page link to share on WhatsApp, Instagram or your Google listing, and the AI books in the chat. Your players stay your own customers, with their games, dues and hour packs kept together on their customer card.

Does iSuite AI – Turf work in Tamil?

Yes for chats: the AI talks with players in Tamil, Tanglish or English. You choose whether it matches each player’s language, keeps to the languages you pick or always uses one. The app’s own screens are in English today. Setup is done with you by the iSuite AI team at MnT Future in Madurai.

Is there a mobile app for turf owners?

Yes, on Android. The iSuite AI app carries the whole product, court board included, and it also works in any phone or computer browser, iPhone too. The desk can take bookings and payments from a phone, and the dashboard shows today’s games, money still to collect, regular teams and the last week’s no-shows.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Both turf plans start with a 7-day free trial. You approve Razorpay auto-debit at the start, and the first yearly payment is taken only when the trial ends; cancel before then and you pay nothing. The plan comes ready with sample courts, rates, an hour pack and desk roles that you edit.
