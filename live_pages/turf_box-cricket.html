For owners

Setting up box cricket slots, prices and rules

Useful whatever software you use — or a notebook. Copy the messages, change the words in brackets.

On this page
  1. Choose a slot length
  2. Write one rate card per box
  3. Night slots: say the night, not the date
  4. Send your booking rules once
  5. Offer office teams a season

1. Choose a slot length teams actually book

Hourly slots are simple to sell and to price. If your regulars keep asking for “one and a half hours”, make that box a 90-minute box instead of juggling half-slots by hand. Keep one length per box, so the board never leaves a 30-minute hole that nobody can book.

2. Write one rate card per box

Look back at a month of bookings — your notebook or your WhatsApp chats — and mark the hours that were always full. Those are your peak. Price them higher, keep the quiet afternoon at the normal rate, and decide whether Saturday and Sunday are one rate all day. Write the advance and the cancellation rule on the same card, so the desk says the same thing every time.

3. Night slots: say the night, not the date

“1 am on Saturday” means Friday night to one team and Saturday night to another, and one of them turns up on the wrong night. Confirm late games by the night they belong to, and count their money with that night’s takings. If you take a higher rate after a certain hour, write when it stops, too. Send the confirmation in the language the team wrote to you in.

4. Send your booking rules once, before the first game

Agree the advance, late arrivals and no-shows in writing before the first game, and there is less to argue about at the desk. Put your rules in one message, send it with each first booking, and keep to it.

5. Offer office teams a season, not a slot

A team that plays every week fills one slot for months. Offer a fixed box and time for 8 or 12 weeks, say what happens on a holiday, and agree how they pay — each game at the desk, or an hour pack bought ahead. If their company needs a GST invoice, ask your CA whether you charge GST on bookings and at what rate before you quote.

What it does not do, said plainly

Not a marketplace

iSuite AI – Turf does not list your boxes on Playo or Hudle, and does not sync with them. Players book through your own booking links and your WhatsApp; enter a marketplace booking on the board as it comes in, and the board blocks that slot. It is a box cricket booking app for owners and staff: players download nothing.

Not there today

  • Tournament fixtures, points tables or team entry forms
  • Partner shares, cash handover or expense books
  • Split payments among the players of a team
  • App screens in Tamil — the AI chats in Tamil, the screens are in English

FAQ

Questions box cricket owners ask

What is box cricket software?

Box cricket software runs a box cricket arena’s bookings and money: each box’s slots on one board, hourly prices with evening and weekend rates, cash, UPI and online advances, and every team’s dues. iSuite AI – Turf adds an AI that answers players on WhatsApp, offers free slots with prices and holds one while they pay.

How much does box cricket software cost in India?

iSuite AI – Turf has two plans: Turf, for one user, at ₹6,999 a year + GST, and Turf with Academy, for two users with a cricket academy’s batches and attendance, at ₹12,999 a year + GST. Both start with a 7-day free trial. No commission per booking: advances paid online go straight to your own Razorpay account.

Can I manage several boxes on one board?

Yes. Add each box as a court and it gets its own column on the court board, with its own slot length, hours, rates and booking link. A clash on any box is refused before it is saved. If one big box is played whole or in parts, booking the whole blocks its parts, and the other way round.

Can players book slots past midnight?

Yes. Set a box to close at 2 am, say, and the 12 and 1 am games stay on that night’s board, so Saturday night’s takings stay with Saturday. The board’s day starts at 5 am unless you change it, and Friday’s 1 am game is priced as Friday’s, not at the weekend rate.

Can I charge more in the evening and at weekends?

Yes. Each box has a normal rate an hour, a peak rate for the hours you choose and one rate for all of Saturday and Sunday. A peak can run past midnight, 7 pm to 1 am for example. Each booking is priced slot by slot when it is made, and a later rate change never reprices it.

Can an office team book the same slot every week?

Yes. Book the team’s box and time once and repeat it for 4, 8 or 12 weeks. Each week becomes its own booking with its own WhatsApp reminders. A week already taken is skipped and named, and “Cancel this and later weeks” calls off the rest of the season and tells the team once.

Can I set 90-minute slots?

Yes. Each box has its own slot length: 30, 60, 90 or 120 minutes. A team books one slot or up to six in a row. Rates are set by the hour, so a 90-minute slot costs one and a half times the rate in force when it starts, whether normal, peak or weekend.

Can players book and pay on WhatsApp at night?

On both plans. The AI answers on your WhatsApp number at any hour, offers only free slots with their prices and holds the chosen one for 15 minutes. With your Razorpay connected, it sends a button to pay what that box asks: an advance (₹500 unless you change it) or the full price. Meta must approve your number first.

How do I reduce no-shows at my box cricket arena?

Take an advance for evening and night slots, mark every no-show on the board and send your booking rules once. In iSuite AI – Turf, a team with two no-shows in six months is asked the full price instead of the advance when it books online, and the desk sees the count while booking them.

Can I run my box cricket arena from my phone?

Yes. The iSuite AI app for Android carries the whole product, court board included, and it also works in any phone or computer browser, iPhone too. From the phone the desk can book a walk-in team, take cash or UPI and mark a no-show, and the dashboard shows today’s games and the money still to collect.

Can I sell hour packs to regular players?

Yes. Set out packs such as 10 hours for 60 days at a price you choose, and sell one at the desk by cash, UPI or card. A game on any box can then be paid with the pack, and its hours come off. A cancelled game gives its hours back; a no-show keeps them used.

Can I run box cricket tournaments with it?

Not as a feature. iSuite AI – Turf has no fixtures, points tables or team entry forms. You can book the boxes for the tournament hours on the court board, so nobody else gets them, and tag the teams that ask as Tournament. For brackets and scores, use a separate cricket scoring app.

Does it sync with Playo or Hudle?

No. iSuite AI – Turf has no link to Playo, Hudle or any other marketplace app today. If your boxes are listed there too, put each marketplace booking on the court board the moment it arrives. That slot is then blocked, so the desk, the AI and your own booking links cannot sell it a second time.

Does it track partners’ shares and expenses?

No. The turf plans keep bookings, the money taken at the desk and online, hour packs and GST invoices, but not partner shares, cash handed over between staff or expense books. The Purchases & books feature is not in either turf plan today, so keep your expenses where you keep them now.
