Careful with clients

Where it fits

iSuite AI – Salon & Spa keeps your appointments, walk-ins, bills, clients, their notes, memberships, packs and the conversations with clients. Salon Basic makes GST bills and receipts. Salon Growth adds the books: expenses, suppliers’ bills, GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B files and a Tally export for your CA.

What the AI receptionist never does

The AI receptionist comes with Salon Growth.

  • Promise results — hair growth, fairness, an end to hair fall
  • Give skin or medical advice
  • Quote a price not on your menu, or offer a discount you have not approved
  • Read a client’s private notes, or share anyone’s details

FAQ

Salon software questions

How much does salon software cost in India?

iSuite AI – Salon & Spa starts from ₹7,999 a year + GST. Salon Basic is ₹7,999 a year + GST for 3 users. Salon Growth is ₹11,999 a year + GST for 6 users, with the AI receptionist and AI credits included. Both are yearly plans with a 14-day free trial. WhatsApp message charges are billed by Meta, separately.

What is the difference between Salon Basic and Salon Growth?

Salon Basic has the front desk, bills, client cards, memberships, packs, the due-back list with a reminder button, payment links and broadcasts, for 3 users. Salon Growth is for 6 users and adds stylist commission, the day close on WhatsApp, reminders on their own, the AI receptionist with AI credits, automations and the books with GST return files.

Can salon software book appointments on WhatsApp automatically?

Yes, on Salon Growth. Its AI receptionist reads your service menu, answers prices and timings, offers the client’s usual stylist and a free slot, and books the service, time and stylist on WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger or web chat at any hour. When it cannot help, it hands the chat to your staff. Salon Basic has no AI agent.

Can clients book a salon appointment online without calling?

Yes, on both salon plans. Each booking calendar has its own booking page, with a link you can put in your WhatsApp or Instagram bio, send in a chat or add to your website. Clients pick a service and a free time, and the booking shows on the front desk board under the stylist whose calendar it is.

Can a salon take an advance payment when a client books?

Yes, on both salon plans, once your own Razorpay is connected. A booking calendar can ask for the full price or an advance. The slot is held while the client pays, confirmed when the money comes in, and released if the time runs out. You could keep one calendar, such as bridal makeup, that asks for an advance.

Can salon software handle walk-in clients and a waiting queue?

Yes. The iSuite AI front desk has a column for each stylist. A walk-in starts with whoever is free for that service, or waits in the queue with a wait time the desk can tell them. Then the visit moves from Arrived to In the chair to Done, the same as a booked appointment.

Can a stylist charge a different price?

Yes. You set the service menu once, with each service’s name, minutes and price, and each stylist ticks the services they do. A senior stylist can have their own price for a service. On Salon Growth, a stylist can also have their own commission rate for it, apart from the service’s usual rate.

Does iSuite AI make GST bills for a salon?

Yes, on both salon plans. A visit’s bill lists each service with the stylist who did it, the products sold, discounts and a member’s discount. With your GSTIN it prints as a tax invoice; without one, as a receipt. You set the GST rate once, take cash, UPI or card in parts, and the receipt goes on WhatsApp.

Can salon software make GSTR-1 files and export to Tally?

On Salon Growth, yes. Its books turn your bills into GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B files for your CA, export your ledgers and vouchers to Tally as XML, and record expenses and suppliers’ bills. Salon Basic makes GST bills and receipts but has no return files or Tally export. Check the figures with your CA before you file.

How is stylist commission worked out?

On what the client paid before GST, after any discount: each service has its rate, and a stylist can have their own. The share is kept on the bill, and a pack’s session earns on the service’s price. Products earn your products rate for whoever sold them. Stylist commission comes with Salon Growth.

How do I remind salon clients when they are due for their next visit?

Set how often each service brings a client back, like a haircut every 30 days, and iSuite AI lists who is due. The desk sends a WhatsApp reminder with a Book my visit button in one tap; on Salon Growth it can also go on its own. Both use your Meta-approved template and skip clients who rebooked or opted out.

Can a salon manage memberships and service packages in iSuite AI?

Yes, on both salon plans. Create memberships such as Gold with 20% off services, see the renewals due each week, and sell packs of sessions such as 6 haircuts or 5 hair spas. On the bill, a member’s discount comes off by itself and a pack’s session pays for the service; voiding the bill puts the session back.

Who can see private client notes like a colour formula or an allergy?

Only the staff you allow: the owner, managers and the stylists you give access to. Notes such as a colour formula, skin type or a PPD allergy sit on the client’s card, and a pinned note shows at the desk when the client books and when they sit down. The AI receptionist never reads these notes.

Does the salon software work in Tamil?

Its AI receptionist does, on Salon Growth: it replies in Tamil, Tanglish, English or the language each client writes in, and you can fix one reply language if you prefer. The app’s own screens are in English. iSuite AI is built by MnT Future in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, and the team sets up your salon with you.

Is there a free plan, or a free trial?

There is no free plan, but both salon plans start with a 14-day free trial. You approve auto-debit with Razorpay at the start, and the first payment is taken only when the trial ends. Cancel before then and you pay nothing. You can also book a free 45-minute demo on WhatsApp first.

Does iSuite AI – Salon & Spa work on a mobile phone?

Yes. It runs in any browser and in the iSuite AI Android app, so the owner can check the board and the bills, and stylists can mark their client in the chair and done from a phone. Calls from your own SIM through the app are logged and can be recorded. There is no iPhone app yet.
