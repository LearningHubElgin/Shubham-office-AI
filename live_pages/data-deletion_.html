User data deletion instructions

Last updated 16 October 2026

About iSuite AI

iSuite AI is a business growth platform developed by Magizh NexGen Technologies. It helps businesses manage customer relationships, Facebook Lead Ads, advertising workflows, Instagram integrations, and customer conversations through connected Meta services.

How to request data deletion

Users can request the deletion of personal data associated with their iSuite AI account and connected Meta services.

To request deletion:

  1. Log in to your iSuite AI account at app.isuite.ai.
  2. If you are using a test account for evaluation, use the test-account credentials provided to you separately.
  3. Contact our support team through the official contact details published on isuite.ai.
  4. Request deletion of your iSuite AI account data and specify any connected Facebook, Instagram, or other Meta-related data you want deleted.
  5. We will verify the request and process the deletion of applicable data in accordance with our data deletion procedures and applicable requirements.

Please do not send passwords, access tokens, or other secret credentials when submitting a deletion request.

What data may be deleted?

Depending on your request and the services you have used, deletion may include:

  • Customer and lead information received through Facebook Lead Ads.
  • Data associated with connected Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts.
  • Relevant customer conversation and integration data.
  • Stored Meta access tokens and connection information associated with the relevant account.
  • Other personal data associated with your iSuite AI account, where applicable.

Disconnecting a Meta account

Disconnecting Facebook or Instagram from iSuite AI stops the relevant integration from being used. Disconnecting an account does not necessarily delete previously stored data. To request deletion of previously stored data, please submit a data deletion request using the process above.

Inactive accounts and deletion requests

For inactive subscriptions, data may be retained for up to 60 days after the subscription becomes inactive, in accordance with our retention policy.

A valid data deletion request is handled separately from the inactive-subscription retention period. We will process applicable deletion requests without undue delay, subject to legal obligations and any applicable retention requirements.

Test account information

Meta reviewers may use the test account provided separately to evaluate iSuite AI’s features. The test account is intended for app review and does not change the data deletion rights or procedures described on this page.

Contact us

For data deletion requests or questions about personal data, please visit:

  • Website: isuite.ai
  • Privacy policy: isuite.ai/privacy
  • Support: Use the official support contact published on our website.
