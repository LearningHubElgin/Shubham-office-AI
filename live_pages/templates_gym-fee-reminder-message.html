Before you copy: fill in the brackets

A gym fee reminder message works when the member can act on it at once: who it is for, which plan, the date, the amount and one easy way to pay. Every message below has those parts in square brackets. Change them, read the message once as the member would, and send it from your gym’s own WhatsApp number.

BracketWhat to put inExample
[Name]The member’s first nameArun
[Gym name]Your gym or studioFit Zone
[Plan]The plan they are onQuarterly
[Date]The day the plan ends, written out12 November
[Amount]What renewing costs, exactly as you charge it₹4,000
[UPI ID]The UPI ID your gym is paid onyourgym@bank
[Offer], [Offer date]Your own offer, if you make one, and its last dayNo joining fee · 30 November
[Freeze end date], [New renewal date]For the freeze message: the last day of the freeze, and the renewal day after it moved20 October · 19 November
[Trainer], [Pack name], [Sessions left], [Last date]For the PT messageVijay · PT 12 · 2 · 30 November
[New end date]After a payment: the day the new term ends12 February
[Festival]The festival or the new yearPongal, Diwali, New Year

Each message comes four ways. Tamil is in Tamil script, for members who read it. Tanglish is Tamil typed in English letters, the way many members chat. Hindi is in Devanagari. A simple rule: answer in the language the member writes to you in. If your prices carry GST, say whether the amount includes it; your CA can tell you the rate that applies to your services.

Membership ending in 7 days

The first gym membership renewal reminder message is a heads-up, not a demand. A week gives the member time to plan the payment and to ask about a different plan.

English

Hi [Name], a friendly reminder from [Gym name]: your [Plan] membership ends on [Date], 7 days from today. The renewal is [Amount]. You can pay at the front desk or by UPI to [UPI ID], or reply here and we will send you a payment link. See you at the gym!

Tamil · தமிழ்

வணக்கம் [Name], [Gym name] சார்பாக ஒரு சிறிய நினைவூட்டல்: உங்கள் [Plan] மெம்பர்ஷிப் [Date] அன்று முடிகிறது — இன்னும் 7 நாட்கள் இருக்கின்றன. புதுப்பிப்புக் கட்டணம் [Amount]. ரிசப்ஷனில் நேரடியாகவோ, [UPI ID]-க்கு UPI மூலமாகவோ செலுத்தலாம்; பேமெண்ட் லிங்க் வேண்டுமென்றால் இங்கே பதில் அனுப்புங்கள். ஜிம்மில் சந்திப்போம்!

Tanglish

Hi [Name], [Gym name] la irundhu oru chinna reminder: unga [Plan] membership [Date] anniku mudiyudhu — innum 7 naal irukku. Renewal fees [Amount]. Reception la direct-a illa [UPI ID] ku UPI la pay pannalaam; payment link venumna inga reply pannunga. Gym la paapom!

Hindi · हिन्दी

नमस्ते [Name], [Gym name] की ओर से एक छोटा-सा रिमाइंडर: आपकी [Plan] मेंबरशिप [Date] को खत्म हो रही है — अभी 7 दिन बाकी हैं। रिन्यूअल फीस [Amount] है। आप रिसेप्शन पर या UPI से [UPI ID] पर पेमेंट कर सकते हैं; पेमेंट लिंक चाहिए तो यहाँ जवाब दें। जिम में मिलते हैं!

Membership ending in 3 days

The gym fees due message. Shorter than the first, with the date and the amount up front, and a line for members who have already paid so nobody feels chased twice.

English

Hi [Name], your [Plan] membership at [Gym name] ends in 3 days, on [Date]. Renew for [Amount] before then and your workouts carry on without a break. Pay at the front desk or by UPI to [UPI ID]. Already renewed? Thank you, and please ignore this message.

Tamil · தமிழ்

வணக்கம் [Name], [Gym name]-இல் உங்கள் [Plan] மெம்பர்ஷிப் இன்னும் 3 நாட்களில், [Date] அன்று முடிகிறது. அதற்குள் [Amount] செலுத்திப் புதுப்பித்தால், உங்கள் பயிற்சி இடைவெளி இல்லாமல் தொடரும். ரிசப்ஷனில் நேரடியாகவோ, [UPI ID]-க்கு UPI மூலமாகவோ செலுத்தலாம். ஏற்கெனவே புதுப்பித்துவிட்டீர்களா? நன்றி — இந்தச் செய்தியைக் கண்டுகொள்ள வேண்டாம்.

Tanglish

Hi [Name], [Gym name] la unga [Plan] membership innum 3 naal la, [Date] anniku mudiyudhu. Adhukulla [Amount] katti renew pannuneenga na, workout break illama continue aagum. Reception la direct-a illa [UPI ID] ku UPI la pay pannalaam. Already renew pannitteengala? Thank you — indha message-a ignore pannidunga.

Hindi · हिन्दी

नमस्ते [Name], [Gym name] में आपकी [Plan] मेंबरशिप 3 दिन बाद, [Date] को खत्म हो रही है। उससे पहले [Amount] देकर रिन्यू कर लें, ताकि आपका वर्कआउट बिना ब्रेक के चलता रहे। रिसेप्शन पर या UPI से [UPI ID] पर पेमेंट करें। पहले ही रिन्यू कर लिया है? धन्यवाद — इस मैसेज को अनदेखा करें।

Membership ends today

The last reminder before the plan runs out. Keep it calm and give a way out: a member who cannot pay today may want a few days, a smaller plan or a freeze, and saying so keeps them talking to you.

English

Hi [Name], your [Plan] membership at [Gym name] ends today, [Date]. To keep training from tomorrow, renew for [Amount] at the front desk or by UPI to [UPI ID]. Need a few more days, or want to switch plans? Just reply here and we will help.

Tamil · தமிழ்

வணக்கம் [Name], [Gym name]-இல் உங்கள் [Plan] மெம்பர்ஷிப் இன்று ([Date]) முடிகிறது. நாளை முதல் தொடர்ந்து பயிற்சி செய்ய, ரிசப்ஷனில் நேரடியாகவோ, [UPI ID]-க்கு UPI மூலமாகவோ [Amount] செலுத்திப் புதுப்பியுங்கள். சில நாட்கள் அவகாசம் வேண்டுமா, அல்லது வேறு பிளானுக்கு மாற வேண்டுமா? இங்கே பதில் அனுப்புங்கள் — உதவுகிறோம்.

Tanglish

Hi [Name], [Gym name] la unga [Plan] membership innaiku ([Date]) mudiyudhu. Naalaila irundhu continue panna, reception la direct-a illa [UPI ID] ku UPI la [Amount] katti renew pannunga. Konjam time venuma, illa vera plan ku maaranuma? Inga reply pannunga — help panrom.

Hindi · हिन्दी

नमस्ते [Name], [Gym name] में आपकी [Plan] मेंबरशिप आज, [Date] को खत्म हो रही है। कल से ट्रेनिंग जारी रखने के लिए रिसेप्शन पर या UPI से [UPI ID] पर [Amount] देकर रिन्यू करें। कुछ दिन का समय चाहिए या प्लान बदलना है? बस यहाँ जवाब दें — हम मदद करेंगे।

Membership expired: come back, no pressure

A gym membership expired message goes about a week after the end date, once. It says you miss them, makes coming back easy, and carries an offer only if you choose to make one. If you do not, leave out the sentence with [Offer] in it; the rest still reads right. The last line lets the member say no, which WhatsApp asks every business to respect.

English

Hi [Name], we miss you at [Gym name]! Your [Plan] membership ended on [Date]. Whenever you want to start again, just reply here and we will set it up. Restart by [Offer date] and you get [Offer]. No pressure at all. Reply STOP if you would rather not get these messages.

Tamil · தமிழ்

வணக்கம் [Name], சில நாட்களாக [Gym name]-இல் உங்களைக் காணவில்லை — மிஸ் செய்கிறோம்! உங்கள் [Plan] மெம்பர்ஷிப் [Date] அன்று முடிந்துவிட்டது. மீண்டும் தொடங்க விரும்பும்போது இங்கே பதில் அனுப்புங்கள், நாங்கள் ஏற்பாடு செய்கிறோம். [Offer date]-க்குள் மீண்டும் சேர்ந்தால் [Offer] கிடைக்கும். அவசரம் எதுவும் இல்லை. இந்தச் செய்திகள் வேண்டாம் என்றால் STOP என்று பதில் அனுப்புங்கள்.

Tanglish

Hi [Name], konja naala [Gym name] la ungala paakave mudiyala — miss panrom! Unga [Plan] membership [Date] anniku mudinjiduchu. Thirumba start pannanum na inga reply pannunga, naanga arrange panrom. [Offer date] kulla thirumba join panna [Offer] kidaikkum. Endha avasaramum illa. Indha messages venam na STOP nu reply pannunga.

Hindi · हिन्दी

नमस्ते [Name], [Gym name] में हम आपको मिस कर रहे हैं! आपकी [Plan] मेंबरशिप [Date] को खत्म हो गई है। जब भी दोबारा शुरू करना चाहें, यहाँ जवाब दें — हम सब तैयार कर देंगे। [Offer date] तक दोबारा जुड़ने पर आपको [Offer] का फ़ायदा मिलेगा। कोई जल्दी नहीं। ये मैसेज नहीं चाहिए तो STOP लिखकर भेजें।

Freeze ending

Send this a day or two before a freeze ends. Its job is to give the member the new renewal date, so the next reminder does not surprise them.

English

Hi [Name], the freeze on your [Plan] membership at [Gym name] ends on [Freeze end date], and your membership is active again from the next day. The days you froze have been added, so it now renews on [New renewal date]. See you back on the floor soon!

Tamil · தமிழ்

வணக்கம் [Name], [Gym name]-இல் உங்கள் [Plan] மெம்பர்ஷிப்புக்கான ஃப்ரீஸ் [Freeze end date] அன்று முடிகிறது; அடுத்த நாள் முதல் மெம்பர்ஷிப் மீண்டும் தொடரும். ஃப்ரீஸ் செய்த நாட்கள் சேர்க்கப்பட்டதால், இனி புதுப்பிக்க வேண்டிய தேதி [New renewal date]. விரைவில் ஜிம்மில் சந்திப்போம்!

Tanglish

Hi [Name], [Gym name] la unga [Plan] membership freeze [Freeze end date] anniku mudiyudhu; adutha naal la irundhu membership thirumba active aagidum. Freeze panna days add aagiduchu, so ippo renewal date [New renewal date]. Seekiram gym la paapom!

Hindi · हिन्दी

नमस्ते [Name], [Gym name] में आपकी [Plan] मेंबरशिप का फ़्रीज़ [Freeze end date] को खत्म हो रहा है; अगले दिन से मेंबरशिप फिर से चालू हो जाएगी। फ़्रीज़ के दिन जुड़ जाने से अब रिन्यूअल की तारीख [New renewal date] है। जल्द ही जिम में मिलते हैं!

PT pack running low

Send it when two sessions are left, from the trainer, in the trainer’s own words. If your packs end on a date, say it: a member would rather hear that sessions are running out from you now than at the desk later.

English

Hi [Name], [Trainer] here from [Gym name]. Thank you for coming so regularly! You have [Sessions left] personal training sessions left in your [Pack name] pack, valid until [Last date]. Shall I keep the next pack ready, so there is no gap? It is [Amount]. Just reply here.

Tamil · தமிழ்

வணக்கம் [Name], நான் [Gym name] ட்ரெயினர் [Trainer]. தவறாமல் பயிற்சிக்கு வருவதற்கு நன்றி! உங்கள் [Pack name] பேக்கில் இன்னும் [Sessions left] பர்சனல் ட்ரெயினிங் செஷன்கள் உள்ளன; அவை [Last date] வரை செல்லுபடியாகும். இடைவெளி இல்லாமல் தொடர, அடுத்த பேக்கைத் தயாராக வைக்கட்டுமா? அதன் கட்டணம் [Amount]. இங்கே பதில் அனுப்புங்கள்.

Tanglish

Hi [Name], naan [Gym name] trainer [Trainer]. Regular-a training ku vandhadhuku thanks! Unga [Pack name] pack la innum [Sessions left] PT sessions irukku, [Last date] varaikum valid. Break illama continue panna next pack ready panni vaikkattuma? Adhu [Amount]. Inga reply pannunga.

Hindi · हिन्दी

नमस्ते [Name], मैं [Trainer], [Gym name] से आपका ट्रेनर। लगातार ट्रेनिंग पर आने के लिए शुक्रिया! आपके [Pack name] पैक में अभी [Sessions left] पर्सनल ट्रेनिंग सेशन बाकी हैं, जो [Last date] तक वैलिड हैं। बिना ब्रेक के जारी रखने के लिए अगला पैक तैयार रखूँ? वह [Amount] का है। बस यहाँ जवाब दें।

Fees received: thank you

Send it the same day the money comes in. It closes the loop, puts the new end date in writing, and tells the member the reminders are done.

English

Hi [Name], thank you! We have received [Amount] for your [Plan] membership at [Gym name]. Your membership is now active until [New end date]. If you need a bill, just reply here. See you at the gym!

Tanglish

Hi [Name], thank you! [Gym name] la unga [Plan] membership ku [Amount] kidaichiduchu. Unga membership ippo [New end date] varaikum active. Bill venumna inga reply pannunga. Gym la paapom!

Hindi · हिन्दी

नमस्ते [Name], धन्यवाद! [Gym name] में आपकी [Plan] मेंबरशिप के लिए [Amount] मिल गए हैं। आपकी मेंबरशिप अब [New end date] तक चालू है। बिल चाहिए तो यहाँ जवाब दें। जिम में मिलते हैं!

Welcome back after a break

For a member who restarts after weeks away, on the day they come back. Warm, short, and pointing them to a trainer rather than to the price list.

English

Welcome back, [Name]! Good to see you at [Gym name] again. Your [Plan] membership is active until [New end date]. After a break, take the first week a little easier — our trainers on the floor will help you plan it. Ask at the desk for anything you need.

Tamil · தமிழ்

[Gym name]-இல் உங்களை மறுபடியும் பார்ப்பதில் மகிழ்ச்சி. உங்கள் [Plan] மெம்பர்ஷிப் [New end date] வரை செல்லுபடியாகும். இடைவெளிக்குப் பிறகு முதல் வாரம் கொஞ்சம் மெதுவாகத் தொடங்குங்கள் — எங்கள் ட்ரெயினர்கள் திட்டமிட உதவுவார்கள். எதுவும் தேவைப்பட்டால் ரிசப்ஷனில் கேளுங்கள்.

Tanglish

Welcome back, [Name]! [Gym name] la ungala thirumba paakradhula romba sandhosham. Unga [Plan] membership [New end date] varaikum active. Break ku apram first week konjam light-a start pannunga — enga trainers plan panna help pannuvaanga. Edhuvum venumna reception la kelunga.

Hindi · हिन्दी

वापसी पर स्वागत है, [Name]! [Gym name] में आपको फिर से देखकर खुशी हुई। आपकी [Plan] मेंबरशिप [New end date] तक चालू है। ब्रेक के बाद पहला हफ़्ता थोड़ा हल्का रखें — हमारे ट्रेनर प्लान बनाने में मदद करेंगे। कुछ भी चाहिए तो रिसेप्शन पर पूछें।

Festival or New Year renewal offer

Pongal, Diwali and the new year are natural times to invite people back. Send this once, a few days before, to members and to those whose plans have lapsed. It is an offer, so it is a marketing message on WhatsApp; the section on template rules below says what that means.

English

When to send each gym fee reminder message

Three reminders are enough: a week before the end date, three days before and on the day itself. More than that starts to read as nagging. After the end date, one message, then let it rest.

MessageWhen to sendKeep in mind

Ending in 3 daysThree days beforeShort; thank those who have paid
Ends todayThe morning of the end dateOffer a few days, a smaller plan or a freeze

Fees receivedThe same day they paySay the new end date
Welcome backThe day they returnPoint them to a trainer

Send between morning and early evening, when the member can pay at once; a message at 11 pm is read as a bother. Stop the reminders the moment someone pays, and if a member asks for a few days, write the new date down and keep to it.

The right tone: clear on the date, kind to the person

  • Say the date and the amount. “Your fees are due” invites “how much?” and a day lost.
  • Make paying easy. Say exactly how to pay — the desk, your UPI ID or a link — in one short line, and offer to help if none suits.
  • Never threaten or shame. No “last warning”, no names read out in a group. Meta lists threatening content and public shaming among the reasons it rejects a template.
  • Keep fees private. Send each member their own message; never post dues in a members’ group.
  • Give a way out. A freeze, a shorter plan or a few days can keep a member who would otherwise quietly stop coming.
  • Sign as the gym. Your gym’s name in the message, sent from the gym’s number, not a trainer’s personal phone.
  • Promise nothing about the body. A reminder is about the plan and the date, not about weight lost or results by a day.

WhatsApp template rules: utility or marketing

Sending a message by hand from your gym’s phone is simple. Sending reminders from software, on the WhatsApp Business Platform, adds Meta’s template rules. These are the ones that matter for a gym, from Meta’s own pages as of October 2026.

  • The 24-hour window. When a member messages you, a 24-hour customer service window opens. Inside it you can send any message; outside it, only a template Meta has approved.
  • Permission first. WhatsApp’s Business Messaging Policy lets you message people only when they have given you their number and agreed to hear from you, and you must respect every request to stop.
  • Utility or marketing. Meta says utility templates must be non-promotional and specific to the person, such as a notice that a payment will be billed on a date. Its guidelines for account and order updates also say they should not include offers or try to secure renewals. Its own example of a marketing message is a subscription expiring with “Renew today to save”.
  • Meta decides the category. Since 9 April 2025, a template sent as utility that Meta finds to be marketing is approved as marketing.
  • Each message is charged. Since 1 July 2025 Meta charges per delivered message, at a rate set by the message’s category and the member’s country code. From 1 October 2026 it also charges for replies inside the 24-hour window, and each business number gets 1,000 free service messages a month.
  • A limit on marketing. WhatsApp may limit how many marketing templates one person receives from businesses; marketing messages sent inside an open 24-hour window do not count towards it. So an offer may not reach everyone.
  • How a template is written. It cannot start or end with a variable, the variables are numbered in order, a short message with too many variables is rejected, and you give Meta sample values for each. Meta says a review can take up to 24 hours.
  • Languages. Meta’s template languages include English (India), Tamil and Hindi, so the Tamil and Hindi messages above can go as templates in their own language.

FAQ

Questions about gym fee reminders

What should a gym fee reminder message say?

A gym fee reminder message should say four things: the member’s name, the plan, the date it ends and the amount to renew. Then give one easy way to pay, such as UPI or the front desk, and invite a reply for help. Keep it short and friendly, and never threaten or shame.

When should a gym send a membership renewal reminder?

Send the first reminder about a week before the end date, a second three days before and a last one on the day itself. Send between morning and early evening, never late at night. Once the member pays, stop the reminders and send a thank-you message the same day.

What should I send when a gym membership has expired?

Wait about a week after the end date, then send one warm message: you miss them, how to restart, and an offer only if you choose to make one. Send it once and let the member say no. If there is no reply, a festival or New Year offer is a better next message than another reminder.

Is a gym renewal reminder a utility or a marketing template on WhatsApp?

Meta decides. Utility templates must be non-promotional and specific to the member, like a notice of a payment date. Meta’s guidelines say utility messages should not include offers or try to secure renewals, so a reminder that pushes renewal, or carries a discount, is likely to be approved as a marketing template.

Can I send gym fee reminders in Tamil or Hindi on WhatsApp?

Yes. Meta’s template languages include Tamil, Hindi and English (India), so a reminder template can be submitted in each of them. For messages you send by hand, answer in the language the member writes to you in. Tanglish suits members who chat in Tamil typed in English letters.
