Careful with members

Where it fits

Not a biometric, turnstile or door-access system, and not a workout or diet app for members. iSuite AI – Gym & Fitness Studio keeps enquiries, members, check-ins, memberships, payments and the conversations with members; on Gym Growth, PT packs, body progress and Accounts for GST invoices and expenses too. Your door hardware stays where it is. It is not yet built for chains with many branches.

What the AI never does

  • Promise weight loss, body changes or results by a date
  • Give medical, diet or injury advice
  • Quote a price not on your plans, or offer a discount
  • Share another member’s details or anyone’s measurements

FAQ

Questions gyms ask

What is iSuite AI – Gym & Fitness Studio?

iSuite AI – Gym & Fitness Studio is gym management software for Indian gyms and yoga, Zumba and CrossFit studios, made by MnT Future in Madurai. Enquiries, members, QR check-ins, memberships with freezes and online payments sit in one app, with an AI front desk that answers plans and books free trials. Gym Growth adds PT packs and body progress.

How much does iSuite AI – Gym & Fitness Studio cost?

Gym Basic costs ₹7,999 a year + GST, and Gym Growth ₹11,999 a year + GST. Growth adds PT session packs, body progress, GST invoices, automations, Meta lead ads and team reports. Both include monthly AI credits and a 14-day free trial: you approve Razorpay auto-debit at the start, and the first payment comes only when the trial ends.

Can the AI book a free trial for my gym on WhatsApp?

Yes, on both plans. When someone messages your gym on WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger or web chat, the AI answers your plans, prices and timings exactly as you set them. It asks their goal and preferred time and books a free trial on your trial calendar. A discount request or a complaint goes to your staff.

How do members check in without a biometric machine?

Print the check-in poster from the app and put it at your door. A member scans the QR, WhatsApp opens with a check-in message, and they send it. They are checked in and told when their plan renews. Members need no app and you need no machine. The front desk can also search any member and tap them in.

What happens when a member with an expired plan arrives?

They are let in, with a warning on the front desk screen. If your Razorpay is connected, they are sent a renewal link on WhatsApp, or the desk is shown the link to share. When they pay, into your own account, the membership renews by itself. Members on a live plan who stop coming appear on a Not seen list.

Does it send gym membership renewal reminders?

Yes, on both plans. Reminders go on WhatsApp before each renewal, on a template Meta approves, with the plan, the date and the price. Your desk sends a Razorpay payment link from the member’s page; when it is paid, the membership renews by itself and the next reminders are set. The dashboard shows renewals due this week.

Can a member freeze their gym membership?

Yes, your staff can freeze it. Each plan can allow some freeze days per term, for example 10 days on a quarterly plan. The renewal date moves by the days frozen, and the reminders move with it. A plan with no freeze days cannot be frozen, and a member who checks in ends the freeze that day.

How are personal training (PT) session packs tracked?

On Gym Growth, sell a pack like 12 sessions in 60 days. Each session is one tap at the desk or on the member’s page, with undo. A pack paid by link starts by itself. Packs running low or ending this week show on one list for your staff to call. Sessions not used by the last day lapse.

Does the AI reply in Tamil, Tanglish or Hindi?

Yes. By default the AI replies in the language the customer writes in, including Tamil, Tanglish, Hindi and Hinglish, or you can fix one reply language. It quotes only the plans and prices you set. It never promises weight loss, never gives diet or medical advice, and hands any discount request to your team.

Does iSuite AI make GST bills for a gym?

Yes, on Gym Growth. It makes GST invoices and quotations on your letterhead, records payments against them, and gives a GSTR-1 export and Tally XML for your accountant. Gym Basic collects payments by Razorpay link and renews memberships, but it does not make GST invoices, so gyms on Basic bill outside the app.

Is there a gym management app for Android?

Yes, for your team. Staff get the whole system in the iSuite AI Android app, with alerts for new chats, and can call members from their own SIM with recordings and AI transcripts. It also runs in any phone or computer browser. Members need no app: they check in and renew on WhatsApp.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API for the AI front desk?

For WhatsApp, yes. The AI on WhatsApp, the QR check-ins and the renewal reminders use the official WhatsApp Business Platform on your gym’s own number, and we set it up with you. Meta approves the number, usually in 1 to 2 weeks, and bills message charges separately. The AI also answers on Instagram, Messenger and web chat.

Can yoga, Zumba and CrossFit studios use this gym software?

Yes. The same edition fits yoga and CrossFit studios, and Zumba and dance fitness classes. It has monthly to yearly plans with freezes, class calendars that hold several people in one slot, QR check-ins and renewal reminders, with session packs on Gym Growth. The AI answers class timings and prices and books a trial class.

Who can see a member’s body measurements?

Only the staff you allow. Body progress is on Gym Growth and is private by default. It records weight, body fat, waist and other measures, works out BMI and shows the change since the first measurement. A trainer taps Send progress to share it with the member on WhatsApp. The AI never shares anyone’s measurements.

What does iSuite AI – Gym & Fitness Studio not do?

It is not a biometric, turnstile or door-access system, and it has no member workout or diet app. It is not yet built for chains with many branches. Your door hardware stays as it is. Members check in by QR on WhatsApp or at the desk, and trainers can send workout plans as files in the chat.
