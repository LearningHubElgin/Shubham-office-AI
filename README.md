# iSuite AI — Enterprise Vertical SaaS Landing Page

A complete, fully responsive, production-ready single-page SaaS landing page for **iSuite AI**, engineered with React, Tailwind CSS, and Lucide React.

## 🚀 Key Features & Architectural Sections

1. **Sticky Navigation Bar (`<nav>`)**
   - Brand Identity: "iSuite A1" with blue gradient geometric abstract icon and "AI OS" badge.
   - Navigation Links: Products, Platform, AI Engine, Comparison Matrix, Workflow, FAQ, and Contact.
   - Action Buttons: "Login" (ghost button) and "Free Trial" (solid primary indigo button with hover glow).
   - Responsive Mobile Drawer toggle with smooth slide down animation.

2. **Hero Section**
   - Deep dark navy/indigo gradient (`#0b0f19` to `#111827`) with ambient glowing lighting effects and grid pattern overlay.
   - Headline: *"Business software made for your industry, with AI built in."*
   - Subtitle: Describing the run-your-whole-business platform across customers, bookings, billing, payments, reminders, and marketing with AI in Tamil, English, and Tanglish on WhatsApp.
   - Interactive Industry Selector Cards (Clinic `iSuite A1`, Coaching Institute `iSuite A2`, Real Estate `iSuite RE`, Gym `iSuite GYM`, Salon `iSuite SALON`, Field Service `iSuite AMC`, and Turf `iSuite TURF`).
   - Starting prices (e.g. `From ₹20,000 a year + GST`) and tier tags.

3. **Dynamic Industry Preview Section ("Built around the way your business runs")**
   - Interactive Tab Bar / Pill Switcher for all 7 industries.
   - High-end split layout:
     - Left Column: Tailored feature lists, Ready-on-day-one modules, and Working numbers.
     - Right Column: Live WhatsApp chat simulation with interactive language switcher (**Tanglish / Tamil / English**), simulated voice notes, typing status, message timestamps, blue double ticks, and quick action reply chips.
     - "Explore [Industry Name]" button and pricing tag.

4. **"Coming Soon" Trades Grid**
   - Section Title: *"Three more trades, on the way"*
   - Subtitle: *"The same platform, made for more kinds of business. Tell us yours, and we will make it ready on WhatsApp."*
   - Cards: Wholesale & Distribution, E-Commerce, and Restaurant & Retail with interactive *"Tell me when it is ready"* notifications.

5. **Core Feature Grid ("Everything your business runs on, in one app")**
   - 6 comprehensive pillars: **AI**, **Customers and Sales**, **Bookings and Operations**, **Payments**, **Marketing**, and **Team and Control**.

6. **Comprehensive Industry Breakdown Matrix / Comparison Table**
   - Interactive switcher between **Ready on Day One** modules vs. **Working Numbers & Capacities** across all 7 industries.

7. **AI Capabilities Showcase ("An AI that works with your team")**
   - 4 deep-dive feature blocks: Multilingual Voice Notes (with Tamil audio waveform UI), Answers on Every Channel, Hands Over with a Brief (AI summary card), and Reminders that go by themselves.

8. **"Live in three steps" Workflow**
   - Step 1: Pick your product
   - Step 2: We set it up with you
   - Step 3: Go live (ready in 24 hours)

9. **FAQ Accordion Section**
   - Fully interactive expand/collapse accordion answering all 12+ common questions with instant real-time search and category filtering.

10. **CTA Banner & Comprehensive Footer**
    - High-conversion dark card: *"Your next enquiry is already waiting. Make sure it doesn't wait for your team."*
    - 5-column comprehensive footer with product links, legal policies (Terms, Privacy, 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee Refund, Shipping, Data Deletion), contact info (`+91 93486 33365`, `info@mktphtech.com`, Madurai, Tamil Nadu, India), and copyright © 2026 Mktph Techcenter Technologies, a unit of Mkt Future.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **Framework:** React 18
- **Bundler:** Vite 6
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS 3
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **Typography:** Inter (via Google Fonts)
